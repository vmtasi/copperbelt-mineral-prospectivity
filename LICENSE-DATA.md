## Third-party sources

The per-cell values are derived from third-party products. The source
products are not redistributed here, and they remain under their own terms.
The CC BY 4.0 grant covers only the authors' own contributions. Cite the
sources as in the manuscript:

- mineral occurrences and permissive tracts: USGS assessment of the Central
  African Copperbelt;
- complete Bouguer gravity anomaly: WGM2012, Bureau Gravimétrique
  International (BGI);
- surficial lithology: USGS Africa surficial lithology;
- fault traces digitized from Selley et al. (2005, Fig. 1b), and tectonic
  domains digitized from Daly et al. (2025). These digitizations are
  derivative works of published figures. Attribution to the original
  publications is required.
