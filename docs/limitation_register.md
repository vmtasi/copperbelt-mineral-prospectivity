# V14 Limitation Register and Scientific Traceability

**Audit date:** 2026-11-05<br>
**Benchmark:** V14 centroid-contact model<br>
**Scope:** every material scientific, reporting and reproducibility limitation identified in the V14 manuscript, its benchmark artefacts and the prior research assessment.<br>
**Mode:** read-only audit. No model was refitted, no MCMC was run, and no manuscript or figure was changed.

This register is the required disposition record for Section 15.6 of the repository mandate and the traceability record for Section 15.7. It deliberately does not claim that the proposed redesign has repaired a historical limitation. A limitation is closed only when the acceptance test named here has been passed and recorded.

---

## 1. Status definitions

| Status | Meaning in this register |
|---|---|
| **Corrected** | The specific defect has been repaired and the repair is verified in the current repository state. |
| **Replaced** | A historical implementation has been superseded by another verified implementation. Historical evidence is retained, but old outputs are not used as current evidence. |
| **Quantified** | The extent or consequence has been measured from existing evidence. This does not mean that the underlying limitation has been removed. |
| **Bounded by available evidence** | The conclusion has been narrowed to what the available evidence can support. The limitation remains but no stronger inference is claimed. |
| **Unresolved** | There is no adequate evidence or artefact, or a required analysis has not been performed. A definitive claim cannot be made. |

**Disposition counts:** Corrected 1; Replaced 1; Quantified 7; Bounded by available evidence 3; Unresolved 27. Total: 39 entries.

`Corrected` and `Replaced` are deliberately rare. The redesign changes the modelling question; it does not retroactively validate V14.

## 6. Unresolved blockers before any claim of completion

The following cannot be closed by repository cleanup:

1. **Structural predictor defensibility (M15).** The current fault layer cannot support a geological hierarchy.
2. **Bedrock/stratigraphic representation (M14).** The active lithology is surficial and class-coded.
3. **Occurrence observation process (M07-M09).** Background and reporting bias remain unresolved.
4. **Slope-versus-intercept identification (M06).** The DVHM comparison does not isolate slope heterogeneity.
5. **Input-uncertainty propagation (M18).** Posterior intervals remain conditional.
6. **V14 uncertainty artefacts (M20, M27).** Calibration, Moran's I and some hierarchical summaries are missing or misattributed.
7. **Reporting consistency (R01-R07, R11).** These require separately reviewable manuscript/figure changes, not silent edits.

These items belong in the redesign's acceptance tests. Passing a new model fit would not, by itself, close them.

---

## 7. Register verdict

The V14 numerical benchmark is reproducible from its preserved OOF predictions, and the north-west Fold 4 result is genuine. The scientific limitations are not erased by that numerical consistency. In particular, the manuscript cannot yet claim that domain-varying slopes are the source of the pooled advantage, that the contact predictor represents bedrock mineralisation, that the fault predictor represents a structural hierarchy, or that posterior intervals represent total predictive uncertainty.

The correct conclusion is narrower and defensible: V14 demonstrates a reproducible application of partially pooled Bayesian logistic regression under a four-sector spatial validation design, with uneven sector performance and a small pooled ranking advantage over a global logistic reference. The redesign must earn any stronger claim with new data and new tests.
